Financial Monitoring Android 1.5.0

Key changes:
- Fixed General Expenses Cancel / Delete confirmation by enabling WebChromeClient in the Android WebView.
- General Expenses can be removed when a purchase is cancelled; deleted snapshots remain in History.
- Added offline payday notifications when today matches a Payday card.
- Added offline due-date notifications for unpaid Payday and Freelance bills/expenses due today.
- Daily reminder check is scheduled around 8:00 AM and is restored after reboot/app replacement.
- Android 13+ notification permission is requested when needed.
- General Expenses now appear as their own value in monthly and yearly Overview summaries.
- General Expenses now have their own column in the January–December breakdown table.
- Total/Actual Spending continues to include General Expenses plus actual Payday/Freelance payments.
- Existing future-payday behavior, allowance logic, backup/import, receivables, and local SQLite data model are preserved.
- App version bumped to 1.5.0 (versionCode 7).

Source-level checks:
- JavaScript syntax parse
- General Expenses monthly/yearly reporting
- General Expenses delete flow
- Reminder scheduling / receiver registration
- Notification permission handling
- Payday and unpaid-due-today filtering
