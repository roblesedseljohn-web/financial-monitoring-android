# One-month stability checklist

Use the app normally, but these checks are especially useful during the trial:

1. Create a payday card with several expenses and different due dates.
2. Mark one expense fully paid, save it, then reduce it to a partial amount and save again.
3. Set a previously paid expense back to ₱0 and confirm the remaining salary increases.
4. Edit a payday date and net pay and confirm the dashboard card updates.
5. Create a freelance income card and attach several expenses.
6. Add a Money Owed to Me entry, record a partial repayment, then increase or correct the repayment.
7. Compare the Overview monthly totals with your actual money received/paid.
8. Open History and confirm create/edit/payment/delete events have timestamps.
9. Delete a test card and confirm it disappears from active totals while its deletion remains in History.
10. Close/reopen the app and restart the phone to confirm records persist.

If any total looks wrong, note the exact sequence of actions that produced it so the next update can reproduce the issue.
